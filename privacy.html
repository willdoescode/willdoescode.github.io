Planet Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: Planet Drop has no accounts and doesn't ask for personal information. Your best score and settings stay on your phone. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who makes the game

Planet Drop is made by an independent developer ("I" or "me" below). You can reach me at williamlane923@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your best score and your sound setting on your device so they're still there next time you play. This information is not sent to me or anyone else, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking; on Android, the Android advertising ID),
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location,
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, such as taps and video views,
  • app performance and crash information.

I don't receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission before ads can use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most phones).
  • Europe, the UK and some US states: where the law requires it, the game asks for your choices through Google's consent message the first time you play. You can change them any time from Menu > Ad privacy choices in the game.

Children

Planet Drop is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, use Menu > Report an ad in the game, or email williamlane923@gmail.com with what the ad showed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: williamlane923@gmail.com. For data Google holds, see Google's privacy controls.
